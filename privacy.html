Privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner, to read and control the owner's own smart-home devices.

It does not collect, sell or share personal data with anyone. Device data is processed on the owner's own computer and is not shared with third parties beyond the services the owner already uses for those devices.

Access granted to this app can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
